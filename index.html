Privacy Policy for FlipRead

Last Updated: November 7, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection

FlipRead is built with strict offline privacy. We collect, store, and transmit zero user data.

  • No Account: You do not need an account, password, or email.
  • No Tracking: FlipRead contains no analytics, cookies, or telemetry.
  • 100% Offline Execution: FlipRead makes zero network calls to external servers.

2. In-Memory Page Capture

When turning a page, FlipRead takes a transient snapshot of the visible viewport strictly in your computer's RAM solely to render the 3D page turn effect. This image is never saved to disk and never transmitted over the internet.

3. Local Storage

FlipRead stores only one local preference on your device: whether sound is muted or unmuted (flipread_muted: boolean).

4. Permissions

  • activeTab: Activates FlipRead only when you click the toolbar icon.
  • scripting: Injects the reading interface into the active page.
  • storage: Saves your mute preference locally.
